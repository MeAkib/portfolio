About

Most of my work sits in the same place: an Angular codebase that has grown past the point where one person can hold it in their head. Multi-tenant billing, enterprise data tools, shared component libraries. The problems are rarely exotic — they are boundaries, bundle size, permissions, and keeping two teams meaning the same thing by the same word.

Before Brain Station 23 I spent two years as a remote frontend developer for a studio in Miyazaki, Japan, and started out converting designs to HTML at an agency in Dhaka. The through line is the same: take something someone drew, and make it work at the sizes and speeds real people use.
